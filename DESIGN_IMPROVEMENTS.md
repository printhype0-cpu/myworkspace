## Design Principles Applied

1. **Modern Gradients:** Used multiple gradient backgrounds for depth
2. **Glassmorphism:** Applied backdrop-blur and transparency effects
3. **Hover Animations:** Scale, translate, and shadow changes
4. **Visual Hierarchy:** Better section labels and size variations
5. **Shadows:** Progressive shadow levels (shadow-lg → shadow-xl → shadow-2xl)
6. **Rounded Corners:** Using rounded-2xl and rounded-3xl for modern look
7. **Spacing:** Increased padding for better breathing room
8. **Typography:** Larger headings with better leading
9. **Decorative Elements:** Blurred circles and background effects
10. **Interactive Elements:** Enhanced hover states throughout

## Animation Effects

1. **Scale Effects:** hover:scale-105, hover:scale-110
2. **Translate Effects:** hover:-translate-y-2, hover:translate-x-1
3. **Shadow Effects:** hover:shadow-xl, hover:shadow-2xl
4. **Underline Animation:** Navigation links (w-0 → w-full)
5. **Fade Animation:** Custom keyframes for smooth entry
6. **Blur Effects:** backdrop-blur-md, backdrop-blur-sm
